What we do

Nine trades, one crew

Hire us for one trade or several. Either way it is our own crew on site, which keeps the schedule honest and the finish consistent.

Kitchens

Cabinet installs, counters and precision finishes. The room the whole house is judged by.

Bathrooms

Tile, vanities and fixtures set with exacting detail. Waterproofing done right, not fast.

Flooring & Interior

Hardwood, trim, built-ins and the interior details that separate finished from done.

Painting

Interior and exterior. Proper prep, clean lines, coatings that hold up to Tennessee summers.

Decks & Outdoor

Decks, porches, wood ceilings and masonry. Outdoor space built to be lived in year-round.

Fireplaces

Custom builds and restorations. The centerpiece of the room, treated like one.

Roofing

Shingle, metal and restoration roofing, flashed and sealed properly the first time.

Framing & Foundation

Block foundations, structural framing and garages. The part nobody sees and everything depends on.

Renovations

Full-home renovations and restorations. Dated and damaged houses taken back to finished.

How it works
