DH Services Home Renovation is the trade name (d/b/a) of Danisa Handyman Services LLC, a residential renovation and finish subcontractor based in Nashville, Tennessee. We work for home builders, general contractors and homeowners.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: your name, company, phone number, email address and job address.
  • Project information: plans, photos, descriptions and messages you send about a job.
  • Call and message records: the date, time and content of calls, emails and text messages with us, including whether you agreed to receive text messages and when.
  • Website data: this website does not use advertising trackers or collect forms. Our host may record standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running.

How we use information

  • To answer your questions and give you estimates.
  • To schedule, coordinate and carry out work, and to send you updates and photos about your project.
  • To send invoices and keep business records.
  • To follow up on an inquiry you or your company made with us.

Text messaging (SMS) and your mobile number

If you agree to receive text messages from us, by texting us first or by telling us on a phone call that we may text you, we use your mobile number only to send conversational, one-to-one messages about your inquiry or project. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. Details are in our SMS Terms.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. All the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run the business (for example our phone and texting provider, email provider and record-keeping tools), only so they can provide that service to us;
  • With the builder, contractor or property owner on the same project, when needed to do the work;
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights and safety.

How long we keep it

We keep records for as long as we need them for the project, for our legal, tax and insurance obligations, and to resolve disputes. Opt-out requests are kept so that we honor them.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including password-protected accounts with limited access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices
